Beyond the game

Real ecological facts

Bees visit flowers for nectar and pollen. Adult butterflies drink nectar from many kinds of flowers. Both can transfer pollen between flowers as they feed.

A real garden does not guarantee visitors after an exact flower count. Visits depend on local species, season, weather, and habitat. Butterflies also need suitable host plants for their caterpillars; nectar flowers alone do not support their whole life cycle.
